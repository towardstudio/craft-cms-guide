The menu on the left of the screen takes you to each area of the CMS. You may not see all of these, depending on what your account is allowed to do.

## Dashboard

The first screen you see after logging in. It can show shortcuts and recent changes.

## Entries

Where most of the website's content lives. Entries are grouped into sections, such as pages or news. Choose a section from the list on the left to see its entries.

[See the sections on {{ siteName }}]({{ guideUrl }}).

## Assets

The library of images, documents and other files used on the website. See [Images and files]({{ guideUrl }}/guide/images-and-files).

{% if not singleUser %}
## Users

Where accounts for people who can log in to the CMS are managed. Most editors won't need this.
{% endif %}

## Other areas

Your website may have extra areas for things like menus, forms and redirects. These are covered under **Extra tools** in the guide's menu.
