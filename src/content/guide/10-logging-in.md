## Where to log in

Go to [{{ loginUrl }}]({{ loginUrl }}) and enter your username or email address and your password.

Bookmark this page. {% if publicGuideUrl %}If you lose the link, go to [{{ publicGuideUrl }}]({{ publicGuideUrl }}). It takes you to the login page, then to this guide.{% endif %}

## If you forget your password

1. On the login page, select **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your username or email address.
3. Check your inbox for an email with a link to set a new password. If it hasn't arrived after a few minutes, check your junk folder.

## Keeping your account secure

- Use a password you don't use anywhere else.
{% if singleUser %}
- This website has one login for the CMS. Only give it to people who need it, and change the password if someone who had it leaves.
{% else %}
- Don't share your login. Ask for an account for each person who needs one, so you can control what each person can do.
{% endif %}
- Log out when you finish if you are using a shared computer.
