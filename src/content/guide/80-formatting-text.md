Rich text fields have a toolbar for formatting, such as bold, italic, links and lists. The options vary from field to field, so you only see what suits that part of the page.

## Pasting from Word or Google Docs

Text copied from other documents can bring hidden formatting with it, which can make the page look inconsistent. To avoid this:

- paste as plain text with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd> (Windows) or <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd> (Mac), then
- reapply bold, links and lists using the toolbar.

## Headings

If the field offers headings, use them in order to give the text a clear structure. Don't use a heading just to make text bigger or bolder. Screen readers and search engines use headings to understand the page.

## Links in text

Write link text that makes sense on its own, such as "read our privacy policy", not "click here".
