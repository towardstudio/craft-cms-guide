---
title: Forms and submissions
plugin: freeform
---

The website's forms are managed in **Freeform**, in the main menu on the left.

## See what people have sent

Go to **Freeform** and choose **Submissions**. You can filter submissions by form, open each one to read it, and export them if needed.

## Email notifications

Forms can send an email notification when someone submits them. To change the recipients the form sends to, click into the form, go to the **Notifications** tab and enter a comma-separated list of the relevant email addresses in the **Recipients** field.

## Personal data

Submissions often contain personal information. Only keep it for as long as you need it, and delete it in line with your organisation's data policy.

Freeform can be set to automatically purge form submissions after a specific period of time. Let us know if that is something you want enabled.
